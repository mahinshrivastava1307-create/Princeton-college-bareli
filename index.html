Admission Guidance

Important Information About Courses

The information below provides general guidance. Official requirements must be confirmed with the college.

Course Duration

Course duration varies according to the programme. Please verify the official duration of your chosen programme with Princeton College Bareli.

Eligibility

Eligibility requirements may differ between programmes. Students should verify the current academic requirements before applying.

Admission Process

The typical admission process may involve enquiry, application, submission of documents, eligibility verification and completion of admission formalities. Follow the official college process.

Documents Required

  • Academic certificates
  • Identification document
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Transfer / migration certificate where applicable
  • Other documents as required by the college

Fees

Course fees should always be verified directly with Princeton College Bareli before admission. Do not rely on unofficial fee information.

Academic Information

Information regarding classes, examinations, attendance, academic support and student development should be confirmed through the official college administration.

For the latest and official admission, course, fee and eligibility information, please contact Princeton College Bareli directly.

Dear Students, Parents and Visitors,

At Princeton College Bareli, we believe that students are the foundation of the future. Education is not simply about gaining knowledge; it is also about developing character, confidence, discipline and responsibility.
